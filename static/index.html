An autonomous research agent designed to discover B2B prospects, extract structured facts from public websites with Playwright, and draft personalized outreach. A data-completeness threshold determines whether it performs a deeper crawl.

The 4 Pillars: Why Results Can Be Trusted

Standard LLMs hallucinate false employee numbers, invent fake founders, and generate generic spam. Here is how our architecture ensures factual integrity:

1. Zero Fabrication Policy

The agent never guesses missing information. If a company like Razorpay does not disclose their exact employee count or founder on the crawled pages, the field is recorded as null / missing rather than inventing a fabricated number like "10-50".

2. Completeness-Threshold Retry

Every lead is scored for weighted data completeness. If it falls below the selected threshold, the agent performs one deeper crawl.

3. Ground-Truth Browser DOM

Rather than relying on outdated static web crawls, the agent launches headless Playwright Chromium to execute client-side JavaScript (React, Next.js, Vue) and uses Trafilatura to extract genuine body text directly from the live site.

4. Evidence-Based Matching

Value propositions are matched only when direct evidence exists in the crawled content. If none of your product's solved problems are supported by the prospect's published text, no match is forced and outreach drafting is skipped.

How the 6-Stage Autonomous Pipeline Works

A step-by-step state machine that mirrors the rigorous workflow of an elite human sales development representative:

1
Intent Search & Discovery

DuckDuckGo search queries find candidate company websites matching your ICP, automatically filtering out noise and directories (like G2, Crunchbase, Wikipedia, LinkedIn).

2
Headless Playwright Scraping

Launches headless Chromium to render the prospect's domain. Extracts semantic markdown and collects internal navigation links (such as /about, /team, /contact).

3
Factual Entity Extraction

When configured server-side, Groq maps retrieved content into strict Pydantic schemas. Outputs remain subject to review.

4
Completeness Scoring & Escalation

Calculates weighted field completeness. If it is below the threshold, Playwright escalates to Depth 1 and re-evaluates coverage.

5
Pain Point Alignment

Compares the company's stated focus and mission against your product's value propositions. Only genuine, evidenced alignments are retained.

6
Outreach Drafting & Self-Critique

Generates concise cold-email drafts from retrieved details, then performs an automated critique and safety check before returning them.

Integrity & Reliability FAQ

Clear answers on data precision, missing fields, and AI reliability:

Why did a company show missing employee size or funding?

If a company does not disclose headcount or funding stage on the crawled pages, the agent reports it as missing instead of estimating it. The completeness score signals missing coverage; it is not a factual-accuracy guarantee.

What does the completeness score mean?

The completeness score is a crawl-depth quality gate. It indicates which weighted fields were filled from retrieved content and highlights fields for manual verification.

How is LLM temperature configured?

Extraction and matching run at a low temperature of 0.3. This minimizes creative drift and prioritizes exact semantic fidelity to the crawled text content.

Can I configure my own product's value propositions?

Yes! Navigate to the Value Props Studio tab to customize the exact customer problems your product solves. The agent will immediately use your updated propositions for all subsequent pipeline runs.
